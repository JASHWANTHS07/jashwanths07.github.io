📖 How to Read a Research Paper (The 3-Pass Method)

Pass 1 (15 min): Abstract, intro, conclusion, headings only. Can you state the contribution?
Pass 2 (1 hr): Read fully, skip proofs. Sketch all figures. Note every equation you don't understand.
Pass 3 (4–8 hrs): Derive every proof. Implement in PyTorch. Re-run their experiments.
Annotate papers using hypothesis.is or PDF annotations. Build a Notion/Obsidian paper database.
Read the related work section like a roadmap — each citation is a prerequisite.
Reproduce the key result within 48 hours of reading. No exceptions.
